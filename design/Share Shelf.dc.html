1Share Shelf: first pass at the core flows
What I'm assuming
One app, two front doors. Parents land on Classrooms need. Staff land on Up for grabs. Everyone can see both tabs.
Sign in with an email link, no passwords. A board member approves each new person once. After that the phone stays signed in.
Posting starts with the camera. Take a photo, give it a name, say when it expires, and it's posted. Needs also get a quantity and an optional link to buy.
Claims are first come, first served. The other person gets an email, and the handoff happens at drop-off or pickup.
Expiration is a set of quick buttons plus a custom date. When a post expires it quietly comes down and the owner gets an email.
Two ways to browse, for comparison: a gallery grid (1c) and swipe cards (1d). Share Shelf is a working name.
