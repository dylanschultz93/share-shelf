# 0008: Before approval: browse with names hidden

**Status:** Decided (2026-10-02)

## Question
What can someone see while they're waiting for approval?

## Decision
- **They can browse** classroom needs and donated items.
- **They can't post or claim anything.**
- **Names are hidden** until they're approved. People show up as "A CECC parent" or "Owl room teacher" instead.

## Why
- Letting new families see what classrooms need is friendly, and it gives them a reason to come back once approved.
- Anyone can type an email address and request access, so "unapproved" effectively means "anyone with the link." Hiding names keeps families' and teachers' identities inside the approved community, which [0007](0007-full-names.md) (full names) depends on.
- It's cheap: one check on how names are displayed.

## What this affects
- Every place a name appears has to check whether the viewer is approved.
- Item photos are still visible to unapproved viewers. That's accepted for now ([principle 2](../principles.md)).
