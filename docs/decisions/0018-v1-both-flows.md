# 0018: v1 includes both the wishlist and up for grabs

**Status:** Decided (2026-10-08)

## Question
What's in the first version: the wishlist, up for grabs, or both?

## Decision
**Both.**
- **Wishlist:** teachers post classroom needs, and anyone can claim them and bring them in (or buy them).
- **Up for grabs:** anyone posts items to donate, and teachers or admins claim them for a classroom.

## Why
- After [0005](0005-roles-by-action.md), both flows end at a classroom and a claim works the same way in either direction. Cutting one wouldn't save much.
- Each flow gives people a reason to open the app. Parents with stuff to give away and parents looking to help both have something to do.
