# 0019: Browse with a gallery

**Status:** Decided (2026-10-08)

## Question
How do people browse needs and donated items: a gallery grid (1c) or swipe cards (1d)?

## Decision
**A gallery for both tabs:** a two-column grid of photos, with the most urgent first and filters for your rooms.

## Why
- It's the most natural way to browse a short list. With about 6–15 open needs, a grid shows everything in a couple of seconds.
- With swipe, "Not me" hides things people might come back for later.

## Later, maybe
A **swipe mode** as an optional second way to browse, like Slack's "Catch up" view alongside its normal layout. It would show the same items, so it's easy to add on top of the gallery if people want it.
