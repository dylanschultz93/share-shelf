# 0003: What it costs to run, and who pays

**Status:** Decided (2026-10-02)

## Question
Can the app cost money to run, and who pays for it?

## Decision
- **Aim for free.** Running costs should be as close to $0 as possible.
- **Trial period:** the founder will cover a small monthly cost for a month or two to see if people actually use it.
- **If it sticks:** the school pays to keep it running, at cost (no markup).

## Why
- A small, unproven tool shouldn't need a budget request to get started.
- Paying "at cost" keeps it fair for CECC as the first school.

## What this affects
- **Prefer services with a real free tier** that covers a school this size.
- **The founder's accounts carry the costs,** and the school reimburses at cost after the trial ([0012](0012-account-owner.md)).
