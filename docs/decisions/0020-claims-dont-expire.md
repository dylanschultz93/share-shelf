# 0020: Claims don't expire, and nobody marks things delivered

**Status:** Decided (2026-10-08)

## Question
What happens when someone claims something and never follows through?

## Decision
- **Nothing automatic.** A claim stays until someone releases it.
- **Either side can release a claim:** the person who claimed it ("Changed your mind? Release it," screen 1f), or the person who posted it ("didn't arrive, reopen it").
- **No "delivered" step.** A claim counts as done. Teachers don't confirm that things arrived.

## Why
- Expiring claims would require someone to mark items as delivered, which is busywork ([principle 5](../principles.md)).
- We trust people, and handle forgotten claims by hand until they're a real problem ([principles 2 and 3](../principles.md)).

## Later, maybe
**Reminder emails** to people who claimed something, before the due date. Nice to have, not v1. Without a "delivered" step, these would be timed reminders ("You said you'd bring 3 Play-Doh by Wednesday"), not "you haven't delivered yet."
