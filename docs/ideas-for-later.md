# Ideas for later

Good ideas we're deliberately *not* building yet ([principle 3](principles.md)). They stay here so they aren't lost and don't creep into v1. Revisit once real people are using the app.

- **Profile photos and a directory.** Optional photos for parents and teachers, so people can put faces to names and use the app as a who's-who for the school. Deferred because a directory is a different job from moving stuff to classrooms, photos slow down sign-up, and pictures of people raise privacy questions. Adding an optional photo to an account later is a small change. *(Raised 2026-10-02.)*
